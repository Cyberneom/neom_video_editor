# Workspace tab dragging — 2026-10-15

The consumer regression is `test/video_craft/video_craft_workspace_tab_drag_test.dart`.
It sends mouse pointer gestures through the actual shared workspace widgets; it
does not call the layout controller to simulate a drop.

Coverage:

- Reorder Timing and Animation within one group, accepting exactly one preference write.
- Move between a dock and a floating group; collapse the empty dock or float.
- Retain a playing preview's mounted state, backend open/pause counts, frame and recipe identity.
- Retain an unapplied title draft and the prepared MP4 download; workspace operations do not add undo entries.
- Reopen a fresh editor with the saved floating group and exact tab order.
- Drop outside the workspace, cancel the pointer or press Escape over a valid target without writing preferences or editing the document.
